How to Play

Goal Grow from Village to Power Land (Level 10). Complete missions to progress. Profile Enter your name at the start. Missions will address you personally. Land Buy land next to owned tiles. Price rises with distance from Town Hall. Mountains can be bought for mines. Terrain Rules Oil Refinery — only on Desert. Ore Mine — only on Mountains. Rivers cannot be built on. Industry Limits Early levels limit how many factories/mines you can build. Limits rise as your city ranks up. Crime & Safety High population without enough Police/Fire raises crime and cuts income. Watch the demand messages. Education Schools, Library, High School, University and Museum raise Education and unlock advanced buildings. Traffic Too many houses and shops without roads or a Train Station reduces income. Big Buildings Airport, Statue and Train Station need 2×2 empty owned land.
